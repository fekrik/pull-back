Privacy Policy

Pull Back: Daily Photo Puzzle. Last updated November 26, 2026.

Pull Back is made by Barq Lab LLC. In short: the app does not collect, store or share any personal information about you.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (results, streaks, statistics and any free play run in progress) is saved only on your device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app downloads

The app downloads each day's puzzle, the free play photos and puzzle data from our server at pullback.barqlab.app, which runs on Cloudflare. Like any web request, this sends your IP address and basic device information (such as the app version) to the server so it can respond. We do not use this to identify you, build a profile, or track you, and we do not combine it with other data. Cloudflare may keep standard technical logs for security and reliability under its own privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of data with third parties.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share, your device's share sheet opens with your result text. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Children

Pull Back is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Photos

Puzzle photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are used under their Creative Commons or public domain licenses. Each photo's author and license are credited in the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. Questions: hello@barqlab.app.

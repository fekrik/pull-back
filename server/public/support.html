Support

Questions, ideas or a photo that seems wrong? Email hello@barqlab.app and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Each day there's one close-up photo. Name the object in six guesses. Every miss, or skip, pulls the camera back to show more of the photo. After three misses you get the category as a hint. Your score is the level you solved it at, so lower is better.

When does the daily photo change?

At midnight in your time zone.

What is Free play?

An endless run of separate photos, so it never spoils a daily. You have three lives, earn points for every photo you name (more for fewer zooms), and the photos get harder as you go.

My guess isn't in the list

Guesses are picked from a list so spelling never costs you. Try a more common name for the object. If you think a word is missing, let us know.

The photo won't load

Puzzles download from the internet. Check your connection and tap Try again. Once a puzzle has loaded, it stays on your device.

Will I lose my streak if I delete the app?

Yes. Progress is stored only on your device, and we have no accounts, so it can't be restored.
